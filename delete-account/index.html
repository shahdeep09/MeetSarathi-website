Delete your account

Effective 17 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your सारथि Sarathi account and the data linked to it. Sarathi is published on Google Play and the App Store by Marudeva Creations LLP.

  • In the app: Settings → Privacy → Delete account, then press and hold Hold to delete.
  • Without the app: email hello@meetsarathi.com with the subject “Delete my account”.
  • Your account and data are erased from our live systems within 30 days, and from our backups within 90 days.
  • We keep a small set of records for longer only where the law requires it. They are listed below.
  • Deletion is permanent. If you want a copy of your data, export it first.

1. Before you delete

  • Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore your journal, reflections or progress afterwards. If you sign up again later, you will start fresh.
  • Export your data first if you want to keep it. In the app, go to Settings → Privacy and choose to export your data. You will get a JSON file with your profile, journal, reflections, settings and consent record. JSON is a plain-text format that you can open in any text editor.
  • Paid subscriptions (if you have one): deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store. Please cancel it in your store’s subscription settings as well.

2. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Sarathi and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, then press and hold Hold to delete until it finishes.

You will be signed out, and your account can no longer be used. Most of your data is deleted straight away, and the rest within the times below.

3. Delete your account without the app

If you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in, you can ask us to delete your account by email:

  1. Write to hello@meetsarathi.com with the subject “Delete my account”.
  2. Send it from the email address registered with Sarathi. If you signed up with a phone number, write from any email address and include your registered phone number.
  3. To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the request from that email or phone number. We will never ask for your password or a one-time code.

We will confirm that we have received your request within 2 working days, and complete the deletion within 30 days of confirming that the account is yours. We will tell you when it is done.

If you can no longer use your registered phone number or email, write to us anyway with any details that help us confirm the account is yours, such as the first name on the account and roughly when you joined. We will delete the account once we are reasonably sure it is yours.

4. What we delete

When you delete your account, we delete:

  • your account and sign-in details, including your phone number, email and password record held by Google Firebase;
  • your profile: first name, age band, gender, language, time zone, path, life profile, and your nudge and journal times;
  • all your journal entries, drafts, mood check-ins and the reflections Sarathi wrote for you;
  • your Karma Mirror trait reflections, level and journey progress;
  • your Ask Sarathi questions and conversation history;
  • the text of any voice recordings (the audio itself is never kept);
  • your consent choices;
  • your devices, push notification tokens and records of the nudges sent to you;
  • your Help-Me requests;
  • feedback you sent in the app; and
  • usage analytics events and app logs linked to you.

5. What we keep, and for how long

We keep a few records for a limited time, and only where the law requires it or where it is needed to show that we handled your request properly. We keep them locked away, use them only for the reason given, and then delete them.

6. How long deletion takes

  • Straight away: when you hold to delete in the app, you are signed out, your account stops working and most of your data is deleted.
  • Within 30 days: everything listed in section 4 is erased from all our live systems, including those of our service providers.
  • Within 90 days: it is gone from our backups. Google Firebase removes its copies of your sign-in details within 180 days.
  • Records the law requires are deleted at the end of the periods in section 5.

7. Deleting some data but keeping your account

  • Usage analytics: go to Settings → Privacy → What you agreed to and turn analytics off. We will delete the analytics events linked to you.
  • Voice: turn off “Speaking instead of typing” in the same place. Audio is never kept, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Notifications: turn them off for Sarathi in your phone’s settings.
  • Particular entries or conversations: email hello@meetsarathi.com and tell us what you would like removed. We will respond within 15 days.
