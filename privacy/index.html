Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what सारथि Sarathi collects, why we collect it, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under Indian law. In short:

  • We collect only what Sarathi needs to work: your sign-in details, a small profile, and what you choose to write or say in the app.
  • We never sell your data, never show ads and never track you across other apps or websites. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID.
  • To write your reflections and answers, your journal and chat text is sent to AI providers. They never get your phone number or email, and they may not use your data to train their models.
  • No one reads your journal or chats, except a small authorised team when you ask us for help or when we must fix a problem.
  • Usage analytics and voice input are optional and stay off unless you turn them on. You can change your mind at any time.
  • You can export your data or delete your account in the app at any time. We erase it from our live systems within 30 days.
  • Sarathi is for adults (18 and over) in India.

Where your data comes from

4. Sensitive information and extra care

Some of what you share with Sarathi is deeply personal. We treat the following as sensitive:

  • your religion and beliefs;
  • your emotional state, mood check-ins and Karma Mirror trait reflections;
  • your journal entries and Ask Sarathi conversations;
  • life circumstances you share, such as losing a spouse;
  • your Help-Me requests; and
  • your password (which only Google Firebase holds, in hashed form).

For this information we:

  • collect it only with your explicit consent, given on the consent screen before any personal question;
  • encrypt it in transit and at rest;
  • let only a small authorised team access it, and only for the reasons in section 8, with every access logged;
  • send AI providers only what is needed to write your reply (see section 6); and
  • never use it for advertising, never sell it, and never use it to make decisions about you outside the app.

5. What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not show ads, and we never share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID.
  • We do not use your journal or chats to train AI models, and our AI providers may not either.
  • We do not use Sarathi to make decisions about you that have legal or similar effects. Karma Mirror and levels are for your own reflection only.

6. How Sarathi uses AI

Sarathi uses AI language models to write your daily reflections, answer your Ask Sarathi questions, find relevant passages in our library of texts, and turn speech into text. To do this, we send the AI providers listed in section 9 only what each request needs:

  • What they receive: the journal or chat text in question, and a few profile details that shape the reply: your first name, age band, gender, language, path, life profile, level, recent mood and trait reflections. For voice input, they receive the audio clip.
  • What they never receive: your phone number, email address, password or account ID.
  • No training: our settings and agreements with these providers do not allow them to use your data to train or improve their models. Requests sent through OpenRouter go only to providers that do not store or train on them.
  • Short-term keeping by providers: some providers may keep a request for a short time, usually no more than 30 days, only to detect abuse and keep their service secure. They then delete it.

AI can be wrong. Sarathi’s replies are generated automatically and may be inaccurate or incomplete. They are guidance for reflection, not religious authority and not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. Our Terms & Conditions explain this further.

7. Your consent and choices

Before Sarathi asks you anything personal, it shows a consent screen. Each choice is recorded with the date and the version you saw.

What is required and what is optional

  • Your journal and your path (required). Sarathi cannot work without processing your profile, journal and conversations as described here. If you do not want this, please do not create an account, or delete it.
  • Usage analytics and crash reports (optional, off by default). Helps us find what is broken.
  • Speaking instead of typing (optional, off by default). Lets you record your voice, which is turned into text.
  • Notifications and microphone. Your phone also asks for these permissions. You can turn them off at any time in your phone’s settings.

Changing your mind

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it. In the app, go to Settings → Privacy → What you agreed to and switch off any optional item. You can also email hello@meetsarathi.com from your registered email, or tell us your registered phone number.

When you withdraw consent, we stop that processing within a reasonable time, tell our service providers to stop too, and erase the data collected under that consent, unless the law requires us to keep it. For example, if you turn analytics off, we delete the analytics events linked to your coded ID. Withdrawing consent does not make processing that happened before it unlawful.

To withdraw the required consent, delete your account (see section 12).

Consent Managers

The DPDP Act lets you give, manage, review and withdraw consent through a Consent Manager registered with the Data Protection Board of India. Sarathi does not yet connect to a Consent Manager. If we add one, we will update this policy and tell you in the app.

8. Who can see your data

People at Sarathi

No one at Sarathi reads your journal or conversations as a matter of course. A small authorised team may see them only when it is needed:

  • to respond to a Help-Me request you made;
  • to answer a support request or complaint you sent us (we will ask you first where we can);
  • to fix a technical problem, or to investigate a security issue or misuse of Sarathi; or
  • when the law requires it.

Each person on this team is bound by a written duty of confidentiality, sees only what the task needs, and every access is logged and reviewed.

Service providers

Companies that run parts of Sarathi for us (section 9) process data only on our instructions and under contracts that require them to protect it at least as well as this policy does.

No one else

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it or to protect someone’s life (see section 18).

10. Processing outside India

Our main database is in India (AWS Mumbai). Some providers above process data in other countries, mainly the United States. We transfer personal data only:

  • to countries that the Government of India has not restricted under section 16 of the DPDP Act;
  • to providers bound by contract to protect it at least as well as this policy and Indian law require; and
  • where it is needed to provide Sarathi to you, with your consent.

If the Government restricts transfers to a country we use, we will stop those transfers.

11. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy. The table in section 3 gives the period for each kind of data. In short:

  • While your account is active, we keep your profile, journal, conversations and settings so Sarathi can work.
  • When you delete your account, we erase your data from our live systems within 30 days. Copies in our encrypted backups are overwritten within 90 days. Google Firebase removes its copies of your sign-in details within 180 days, as Google states.
  • If you stop using Sarathi, we treat an account as inactive after 24 months with no sign-in or use. We will send you a notice at least 30 days before we delete it, by email, app notification or SMS, using whichever contact details we have. If you do not sign in or contact us during that time, we delete the account as described above.
  • What the law makes us keep. We keep a small set of records for longer only when Indian law requires it or to show that we handled your requests properly:
    • your registered phone number or email and account ID, with security logs, for up to 180 days after deletion (IT Rules 2021 and CERT-In Directions 2022);
    • processing logs for up to one year from the date of processing, once the relevant DPDP Rules apply (from May 2027);
    • records of your requests and complaints, for up to 2 years after the matter is closed; and
    • billing and tax records if you buy a paid plan, for as long as tax and accounting laws require (at present up to 8 years).
    We keep these locked away, use them only for that purpose, and delete them when the period ends. Our Delete your account page lists them in full.

Information that has been made anonymous, so that it can no longer identify anyone, such as “1,000 journals were written this week”, is not personal data and may be kept.

12. Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: in the app, go to Settings → Privacy and choose to export your data. You will get a JSON file with your profile, journal, reflections, settings and consent record.
  • Delete in the app: go to Settings → Privacy → Delete account and press and hold Hold to delete. This is permanent.
  • Delete by email: write to hello@meetsarathi.com with the subject “Delete my account”, from your registered email or giving your registered phone number.

Our Delete your account page explains exactly what is deleted, what is kept, and for how long.

13. How we protect your data

We follow reasonable security practices, as required by section 43A of the IT Act, the SPDI Rules and the DPDP Rules. These include:

  • encrypting data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest;
  • giving access only to people who need it, with strong sign-in and access logs that are kept and reviewed;
  • monitoring for unauthorised access and investigating it;
  • regular, encrypted backups so the service can recover from failures;
  • a documented information security programme and incident response plan; and
  • contracts that require our service providers to keep data secure.

No system is perfectly secure. Please keep your phone locked and your password private, and tell us at once if you think someone else has accessed your account.

14. If a data breach happens

If your personal data is affected by a breach, we will tell you without delay, through the app, by email or by SMS. We will explain in plain language:

  • what happened, when, and how much data was involved;
  • what it may mean for you;
  • what we are doing to limit the harm;
  • what you can do to protect yourself; and
  • who to contact with questions.

We will also report the breach to the Data Protection Board of India, as the DPDP Rules require (first without delay, then in full within 72 hours), and to CERT-In within six hours of noticing a reportable cyber incident.

15. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access: get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we process it, and the names of other Data Fiduciaries and Data Processors we have shared it with, with a description of what was shared. The in-app export gives you a full copy.
  • Correct and update: fix data that is wrong, incomplete or out of date. You can change most of your profile in the app.
  • Erase: ask us to delete your data, or delete your account in the app.
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, as explained in section 7.
  • Grievance redressal: complain to our Grievance Officer (section 19) and get a response.
  • Nominate: name a person who can exercise your rights if you die or become unable to act yourself.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India: if you are not satisfied with our response to your grievance.

How to make a request

Email hello@meetsarathi.com from your registered email, or give your registered phone number, and tell us what you would like. Your registered phone number or email is the identifier we use to find your account. To protect you, we may ask you to confirm the request from that email or phone. We will never ask for your password. Making a request is free.

We acknowledge requests within 24 hours and respond within 15 days. Every reply includes the contact details of our Grievance Officer.

Nominating someone

To nominate someone, email us their name, their relationship to you, and their email or phone number. If you die or become unable to act, your nominee can contact us with proof (for example a death certificate or medical certificate) and their own ID, and we will act on their request as the law allows.

Your duties

The DPDP Act also asks you to give accurate information, not to impersonate anyone, and not to file false or frivolous complaints.

16. Adults only

If we learn that someone under 18 has created an account, we will delete the account and its data. If you think a child is using Sarathi, please tell us at hello@meetsarathi.com.

If you are the lawful guardian of an adult with a disability who would like to use Sarathi, please contact us first, so we can obtain your verifiable consent as the law requires.

18. When we may disclose data

  • If Sarathi changes hands: if Sarathi is transferred to another organisation (for example as part of a restructuring), your data would move only under the same protections. We will tell you in advance, so that you can export your data or delete your account if you wish.
