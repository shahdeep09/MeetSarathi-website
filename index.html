What is सारथि Sarathi?

In the old stories, the sarathi is the charioteer: the one who keeps the reins steady while the warrior faces the battle.

Sarathi does the same for the battle inside, with anger, pride, worry and old habits. It is rooted in Jain psychology and spoken in everyday language, so it fits into an ordinary day at home, at work, or anywhere in between.

It does not grade you, chase streaks or raise its voice. It helps you notice, and offers one small step at a time.

What you will find inside

Each part is small on purpose. Together they make a daily rhythm: a teaching to begin, a few pauses along the way, and a moment to look back.

  • A daily wisdom card

    Each morning, one short teaching in your language, drawn from a library of about 250 books on Jain thought and told in everyday words.

  • Gentle nudges, quiet nights

    Up to seven short nudges a day, at times that fit your routine. Nothing arrives between 10 pm and 7 am.

    Five questions, about four minutes, typed or spoken. Sarathi reads your day back to you with a short reflection and one small step for tomorrow.

How it works

Three small moments a day. Nothing more is asked of you.

  1. 1

    Set up in a few minutes

    Choose your language, pick the life profile closest to yours, and set the times that suit you for nudges and the evening journal.

  2. 2

    Go about your day

    A teaching arrives in the morning. A few gentle nudges follow, each one a small pause to notice, breathe and choose again.

  3. 3

    Reflect at night

    Answer five short questions, typed or spoken. Sarathi reads your day back to you and offers one small step for tomorrow.

For the first fifteen days, Sarathi mostly listens, so that what it offers later fits you.

Your reflections are yours.

Sarathi is built for honest moments, so it has to be a safe place to have them.

Questions, answered

Not finding what you need? Write to hello@meetsarathi.com or visit the support page.

Is सारथि Sarathi free?

Yes, Sarathi is free to use right now. Paid plans may come later. If they do, the price, any free trial and how renewal works will be shown in the app or the store before you decide to pay.

Who can use it?

Adults aged 18 and over. Sarathi is rooted in Jain teaching but written in everyday language, so anyone who wants a steadier inner life is welcome.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Hindi (हिन्दी) and Gujarati (ગુજરાતી). You choose when you set up, and teachings, the journal and answers all follow your choice.

Is Sarathi therapy or medical care?

No. Sarathi offers spiritual and reflective guidance only. It is not medical, psychological or psychiatric care, and it cannot respond to emergencies.

If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a qualified professional. In India, Tele-MANAS is free and open 24 hours a day: call 14416. In an emergency, call 112.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, open Settings, then Privacy, and choose Delete account. Hold the button to confirm. Your account and data are removed within 30 days.

You can also write to hello@meetsarathi.com with the phone number or email you signed in with. The full steps are on the delete account page.

When can I download it?

Sarathi is being tested now and will arrive on Google Play and the App Store soon. This page will link to both as soon as it does.
